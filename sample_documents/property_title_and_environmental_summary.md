## 4. PHASE I ENVIRONMENTAL SITE ASSESSMENT (ESA)

Assessment conducted in accordance with ASTM E1527-21 standard practice by GeoTech Environmental Consultants Inc.

### 4.1 Historical Land Use
- 1945–1988: Agricultural hay and timber storage.
- 1989–2017: Vacant unimproved pastureland.
- 2018–Present: Light staging yard for non-hazardous shipping containers.

### 4.2 Environmental Findings
- **Recognized Environmental Conditions (RECs):** **NONE IDENTIFIED.** No evidence of historical underground storage tanks (USTs), polychlorinated biphenyls (PCBs), or chemical disposal.
- **Historical RECs (HRECs):** None.
- **Controlled RECs (CRECs):** None.
- **Groundwater & Soil Vapor:** Five test borings conducted on September 28, 2026 detected all volatile organic compounds (VOCs) and heavy metals well below EPA Region 4 Industrial Screening Levels.
- **Flood Zone Designation:** Zone X (Area of Minimal Flood Hazard, outside 500-year floodplain). FEMA Map Panel #13051C0185F.

### 4.3 Conclusion & Recommendation
The environmental consultant concludes that no further subsurface investigation (Phase II ESA) is warranted at this time.
