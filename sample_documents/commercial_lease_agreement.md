# COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

**PROPERTY:** 742 Evergreen Plaza, Suite 400, Metro City, CA 94107  
**EFFECTIVE DATE:** November 1, 2026  
**DOCUMENT ID:** CLA-2026-742E-04  

---

## PARTIES

This Commercial Lease Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between:

1. **LANDLORD:** Apex Commercial Properties LLC, a California limited liability company, with its principal office at 500 Financial Boulevard, Suite 1200, San Francisco, CA 94104 ("Landlord").
2. **TENANT:** Horizon Health & Wellness Inc., a Delaware corporation registered to do business in California, with principal offices at 742 Evergreen Plaza, Suite 400, Metro City, CA 94107 ("Tenant").

---

## 1. DEMISED PREMISES

Landlord leases to Tenant, and Tenant rents from Landlord, approximately **6,850 rentable square feet** of commercial office and clinical suite space located on the 4th Floor of the building commonly known as Evergreen Plaza, Suite 400 ("Premises").

---

## 2. TERM & COMMENCEMENT

- **Initial Term:** Five (5) years (60 calendar months).
- **Commencement Date:** November 1, 2026.
- **Expiration Date:** October 31, 2031.
- **Renewal Option:** Tenant holds an option to extend this Lease for one (1) consecutive term of three (3) years upon written notice provided at least one hundred eighty (180) days prior to the Expiration Date. Base rent during the option term shall be negotiated at ninety-five percent (95%) of Fair Market Value (FMV).

---

## 3. RENT, ESCALATION & SECURITY DEPOSIT

### 3.1 Base Rent
Tenant agrees to pay Landlord an initial Base Rent of **$14,500.00 per month** ($174,000.00 annualized, equivalent to $25.40/sq ft/year), payable on or before the first (1st) day of each calendar month.

### 3.2 Annual Rent Escalation
Beginning on the first anniversary of the Commencement Date (November 1, 2027), and every twelve (12) months thereafter, the monthly Base Rent shall increase by **three and one-half percent (3.5%)** over the previous year's Base Rent.

- Year 1 (Nov 2026 - Oct 2027): $14,500.00 / month
- Year 2 (Nov 2027 - Oct 2028): $15,007.50 / month
- Year 3 (Nov 2028 - Oct 2029): $15,532.76 / month
- Year 4 (Nov 2029 - Oct 2030): $16,076.41 / month
- Year 5 (Nov 2030 - Oct 2031): $16,639.08 / month

### 3.3 Security Deposit
Upon execution of this Lease, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord the sum of **$29,000.00** (representing two months' Base Rent) as a Security Deposit to ensure faithful performance of all covenants herein.

---

## 4. PERMITTED USE & OPERATING HOURS

The Premises shall be used solely for outpatient medical consultation, wellness therapy, and administrative support services, and for no other purpose without prior written consent from Landlord.
- **Normal Operating Hours:** Monday through Saturday, 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM.
- Tenant shall have 24/7 keycard access to the building and elevator lobbies.

---

## 5. MAINTENANCE AND REPAIRS

### 5.1 Landlord Obligations
Landlord shall, at its sole cost and expense, maintain and keep in good repair:
- The building foundation, exterior walls, and roof structure.
- Main building mechanical, plumbing, electrical, and HVAC distribution mains up to the perimeter boundary of the Premises.
- Common areas, parking lots, exterior landscaping, and shared passenger elevators.

### 5.2 Tenant Obligations
Tenant shall, at its own cost, maintain the interior of the Premises in good order and condition:
- Interior non-structural partitions, fixtures, lighting ballasts, and floor coverings.
- Dedicated interior plumbing fixtures within Suite 400.
- Routine maintenance of supplementary package HVAC units servicing the medical testing room.

---

## 6. INSURANCE REQUIREMENTS

Tenant shall maintain throughout the Term:
- **Commercial General Liability Insurance:** With a combined single limit of not less than **$2,000,000.00 per occurrence** and $4,000,000.00 general aggregate.
- **Worker's Compensation:** Meeting statutory state limits.
- **Property Insurance:** Special form coverage insuring all trade fixtures, equipment, and personal property at full replacement value.
- Landlord and its property management agent shall be named as Additional Insureds.

---

## 7. DEFAULTS AND REMEDIES

Any of the following constitutes an Event of Default by Tenant:
1. **Monetary Default:** Failure to pay Base Rent or Additional Rent within **five (5) business days** after written notice that payment is overdue. A late fee of five percent (5%) shall apply.
2. **Non-Monetary Default:** Failure to comply with any other provision of this Agreement within **fifteen (15) calendar days** after written notice from Landlord (or such reasonable longer time if cure has commenced diligently).

---

## 8. ASSIGNMENT AND SUBLETTING

Tenant shall not assign this Lease or sublease any portion of the Premises without Landlord's prior written consent, which consent shall not be unreasonably withheld, conditioned, or delayed. Any transfer resulting from merger or internal corporate reorganization shall not require Landlord consent provided the net worth of the surviving entity equals or exceeds Tenant's net worth as of the Effective Date.
