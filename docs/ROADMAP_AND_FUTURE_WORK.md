# Product Roadmap and Future Architecture

Future technical initiatives and architectural enhancements planned for the Property Intelligence Platform.

## 1. Planned Enhancements

### Phase 17: Autonomous Multi-Agent Legal Due Diligence
- Deploy collaborative LLM agents specialized in distinct legal checks (Title Examiner Agent, Encumbrance Auditor Agent, Revenue Verification Agent).
- Generate automated legal opinion summaries in PDF format with digital watermarking.

### Phase 18: Streaming RAG with Progressive Citation Highlighting
- Implement Server-Sent Events (SSE) / WebSockets for sub-second streaming answers to user inquiries.
- Stream progressive token citations to immediately highlight corresponding passages in the original PDF viewer.

### Phase 19: Multimodal Map & Survey Boundary Verification
- Integrate computer vision models to compare cadastral map boundaries with municipal master plans.
- Automatically identify road setbacks and zoning buffer violations.

### Phase 20: Blockchain-Anchored Audit Trail
- Anchor document SHA-256 hashes and timestamped audit logs onto public/consortium distributed ledgers for tamper-proof evidentiary proof.
